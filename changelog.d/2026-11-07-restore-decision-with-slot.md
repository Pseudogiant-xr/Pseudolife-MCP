### Fixed (2026-11-07 — a world or lesson restore never records its decision without the restored value)
- `memory_world_restore` and `memory_lesson_restore` (and the Console's restore actions) wrote the restore decision and saved the restored slot in two separate commits. When the database connection was lost at the slot save's commit, the decision stayed in the bank while the restored value did not, so the review history claimed a restore that never happened, and that persisted across a restart. Both engines now commit the decision and the slot save in one transaction: a lost commit leaves both or neither, and a failed save marks the slot for the next save again.
