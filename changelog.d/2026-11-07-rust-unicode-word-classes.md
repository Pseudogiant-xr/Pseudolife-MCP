### Fixed (2026-11-07 — Rust daemon classifies non-ASCII text with CPython's own Unicode tables)
- Four text tests in the Rust daemon used approximations of Python's Unicode classes and now use CPython 3.11's own tables: the pin-scope word boundary in cortex search (combining marks such as Thai tone marks continue a word), the generic session-title shape (non-ASCII decimal digits count, as Python's `\d` does), the deep dream's variant-token boundaries (dotted and dotless I are letters under `re.IGNORECASE`) and its identifier-keyed entity pattern (`\w`, `\S`, and `$` before a final newline).
