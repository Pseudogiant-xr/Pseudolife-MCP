### Fixed (2026-11-08 — the Rust daemon's deep dream no longer holds every candidate pair in memory)
- The Rust daemon's full-corpus deep dream ranked link and merge candidates, and lesson and world-fact duplicates, by collecting every pair above the similarity floor, sorting them, and only then keeping the top `top_k_candidates` / `curation_top_k`. With the floors lowered, its memory grew with every surviving pair: on a 13,000-entity bank with every floor at -1.0, one `dream deep` passed 18 GiB and was killed, where the Python daemon finished in 9.2 GiB. Both passes now keep only the best `top_k` pairs as they scan, and build labels for the kept pairs only. The output is unchanged, ties included. At the default floors the same bank's deep dream peaked at 4.4 GiB before and is unaffected.
