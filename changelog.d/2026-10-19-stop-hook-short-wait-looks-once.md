### Fixed (2026-10-19 — a short Stop-hook wait still looks for mail once, and ends at its deadline)

- `plugin/hooks/stop-wake.sh` always takes its first look at the digest. Before, a `PSEUDOLIFE_AGENT_WAKE_HOOK_WAIT` shorter than the hook's own start-up (`SECONDS` counts it, and Git Bash spawns cost seconds on a loaded Windows host) gave up before looking, so mail already waiting was missed. The last sleep is also cut to the time left, so a short wait ends at its deadline instead of a whole 5 s poll later. With the default 14-day wait neither change is visible. The test file `tests/test_stop_wake_hook.py` now runs in 119 s instead of 297 s in one process (2026-10-19, WSL): its "never wakes" tests used 8 s waits so that a slow start could not skip their look, and they now use 1 s ones. The plugin cache moves with the client step (`pseudolife-mcp update`, or `python ops/update_clients.py` from a checkout); `plugin/hooks/hooks.json` is unchanged, so Codex asks for no new approval.
