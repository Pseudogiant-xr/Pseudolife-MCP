### Fixed (2026-11-24 — a native-client reinstall right after the Docker uninstall is no longer refused with a false "port in use") [#1905]
- The native installer's port check no longer counts a port that only holds a stopped service's closed connections (TIME-WAIT) as "already in use by another service". On Linux such a port now passes when the daemon's publication could bind it. Where a port still cannot be bound but no service answers on it, the refusal says so: it was most likely used a moment ago by a service that has stopped, so rerun in about a minute. Ports a service listens on are refused as before, and Windows, where TIME-WAIT never blocked the check, is unchanged. The Python client's installer check (`ops/preflight.py`) is not changed.
