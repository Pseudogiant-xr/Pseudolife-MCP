### Fixed (2026-11-07 — Rust daemon re-reads the bank after a write whose commit outcome is unknown)
- When the database connection was lost while a write's COMMIT was in flight (or the COMMIT failed), the Rust daemon answered that call with an error but kept serving its in-memory copy, which could disagree with what the bank actually committed until the daemon restarted: a supersede, consolidate, reinstate or forget that had committed was not visible to later searches. The next call now re-reads the bank before it is served (the stores, the episode log and the dream's acknowledgement state), after the writes already running have finished, and keeps retrying the re-read on later calls until one succeeds. Access counts searches accrued since the last autosave are kept; a fact, world fact or lesson change whose save had already failed is dropped, as a restart drops it. The Python daemon reaches the same state through its per-operation recovery checks.
