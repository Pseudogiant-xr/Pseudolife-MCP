### Fixed (2026-11-24 — Codex hook launchers name a step an installed client has) [#1609]
- The Codex hook launchers (`run.sh`, `run.ps1`) now say "rerun the installer and approve the Codex hooks" when their bundle pointer is broken, instead of the checkout-only `python ops/setup-codex-hooks.py`.
- No hook re-approval is expected: Codex approves the command that names a launcher, and that command and every hook definition (`plugin/hooks/hooks.json`, the manual hook commands) are unchanged; Codex does not hash the launcher's bytes.
- An approved installer rerun and `pseudolife-mcp update` (release and checkout mode) replace the launchers every earlier release shipped (their sha256 is in `PREVIOUS_LAUNCHERS`); checkout-mode `update` refuses a launcher no release shipped as modified, and release-mode `update` leaves one for setup to report. A release-mode `update` that cannot replace a launcher (one held open, a permission) reports why and says to rerun `pseudolife-mcp update --clients-only`, instead of failing the Codex step.
- Version skew: a client from before this release does not know these launcher bytes. That covers an older `pseudolife-mcp`, a rollback with `pseudolife-mcp update --tag <older>`, or an older checkout. Its `doctor`, installer verification and checkout-mode `update` report the launchers as missing or modified. Update to this release or later, or rerun that older release's installer and approve the Codex hooks, which rewrites the launchers to its own bytes.
