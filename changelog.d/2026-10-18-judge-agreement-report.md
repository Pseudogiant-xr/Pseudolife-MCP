### Added (2026-10-18 — a judge-agreement report, the number a shadow judge mode is promoted on)
- A judge mode in `shadow` records votes and applies nothing, and nothing scored those votes against the decisions that followed: the v56 link second opinion and merge tiebreak shipped in shadow with no path out of it, and the merge second opinion before them was promoted on a ladder panel from another month. `dream_status` (`memory_dream(action="status")`, `GET /api/dream/status`) now carries `review_queue.judge_agreement`: every recorded vote scored against the decision a human (Console) or agent (`memory_graph_review`) later took on the same row, over the last 90 days. Per queue (`merge`, `junk`, `link`) and opinion (`judge`, `judge2`, `judge3`): `voted`, `agree`, `abstain`, `precision` and `by_verdict`; `agreed_two` (merge and link) scores the rows whose first two opinions agreed from distinct models at the two-vote gates, and `majority_three` the rows whose 2-of-3 majority cleared its gate — what `auto` would have applied. `scored` counts the decisions read and `labels` who took them; decisions the judges applied themselves (`dream-judge`, `dream-auto`) are not labels. "Distinct models" follows the live gates: every vote in a pair or majority carries the model its endpoint reported serving (the first opinion's from the judging memo), the names differ and so do the configured stamps; a vote with no served name is never part of a pair. Not replayed: the `low_differential` exclusion (computed from the live graph at judging time), so the accept side of `agreed_two` and `majority_three` counts a few rows `auto` would skip; and a decided link row goes with either endpoint when a later merge or junk delete takes it, so links are scored while both endpoints exist. The block never fails the status call (a failure is an `error` field); `MemoryService.judge_agreement(days=...)` widens or narrows the window.
- Every merge and junk decision — accept, reject, keep, the dream's unattended junk delete — now copies the proposal's judge votes onto its FK-free audit row first, into the `merge_decisions.votes` column schema v56 added for it: per opinion the verdict, confidence, model, served model (when the endpoint reported one) and the relation a relate vote named. An accepted merge CASCADE-deletes its proposal row with the votes on it, which is why the audit row holds them. NULL = a decision from before this release, or one on a row no judge had seen; neither is scored. Link decisions leave their votes on the `edge_proposals` row and are scored from there (links decided before this build carry no `decided_by` and are counted as `unattributed_links`, not scored).
- Link decisions are attributed. The Console's `accept-proposal` / `reject-proposal` routes and `memory_graph_review`'s `accept_link` / `reject_link` settled a link with no `decided_by` (so the report could score none of them); they now record `human` (the Console's default, or the `decided_by` it sends) and `agent`, and the dream's untyped-edge retype pass names its rejects `dream-auto`. Rows decided before this build keep NULL and are reported as `unattributed_links`, not read as a human's.
- The `/dream` command reports the number beside the queue counts while a judge mode is in `shadow`; the deep-dream runbook names it as the promotion step.
