### Fixed (2026-11-10 — Rust daemon's autosave saves canonical facts a call left unsaved)
- The Rust daemon's autosave (every 30 s by default, and the save at a clean exit) wrote only the counters row and access counts. Two cortex changes are not saved by the call that makes them: a `memory_fact_set` whose own save failed (the call answers the error, the value stays in memory), and `memory_forget`'s cascade retiring a slot whose last source entry was forgotten (its `retired` supersession-log row). In the Rust daemon they stayed unsaved until the next write to that store, so a restart before one lost them; the Python daemon saves them within one autosave or at exit. The Rust autosave now saves every canonical store (cortex, world facts, lessons) that holds unsaved changes, under the writer lock, and a failed save leaves them marked for the next autosave to retry. An idle or read-only daemon still writes nothing.
