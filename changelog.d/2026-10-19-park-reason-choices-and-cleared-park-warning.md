### Fixed (2026-10-19 — a wrong park reason is refused with the accepted ones, and a status that clears a park says so)
- `memory_agents(action="update")`'s schema now lists `park_reason`'s choices (`done`, `blocked`, `needs_approval`, `needs_info`, `needs_resource`, `waiting_peer`, or `""` to clear). A value outside them is refused with that list. Before, a long value was refused with "String should have at most 20 characters", and only a second guess showed the real choices.
- A status update without park fields still clears a standing park: a session that is working is not parked, so its mail is hinted instead of decided on a stale park. The update's result now carries a `warning` naming the cleared reason and saying to send the park fields with the status to stay parked. Before, the park went silently, and a session that meant to stay parked lost its wake path. The REST update answers the same way, and so does the Rust daemon.
