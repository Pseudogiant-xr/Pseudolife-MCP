### Fixed (2026-10-24 — a stale census in a batch holds back the members that cost nothing to wait, not the census changers)

- When CI's producer census check would fail on a batch head, `python ops/integrate.py build` used to drop every member whose own diff changes the census inputs, although such a member's census is usually current on master and goes stale only because other members change files the census hashes. Its author then had to merge master forward, regenerate the census and relabel; #873 and #881 went through that in consecutive batches (2026-10-23/24). `build` now first checks that the census changers pass the check on their own, then merges the batch again without the other members whose own diff touches a hashed path (what master's census module lists in the batch tree, plus the paths master's and each changer's committed census hash), searching each trial head again for members a conflict had kept out. If that head passes it is the batch: those members are listed with the reason in the batch pull request under `## Waiting (no action needed)`, get no comment, keep their `ready-to-batch` label and join the next batch, where CI only reports the census. Otherwise the census changers are dropped as before.
