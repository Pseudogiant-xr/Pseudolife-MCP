usage: pseudolife-mcp export [-h] [--out OUT] [--data-dir DATA_DIR]

Write a portable logical export of the bank (ZIP of JSONL tables + manifest).

options:
  -h, --help           show this help message and exit
  --out OUT            destination .zip (default: ./pseudolife-
                       export-<ts>.zip)
  --data-dir DATA_DIR  bank data dir for the lite tier (default:
                       PSEUDOLIFE_MCP_DATA_DIR, the lite per-user dir, or
                       ./data)
