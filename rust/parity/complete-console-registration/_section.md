## Complete Console registration checklist

Shared source: pseudolife_memory/web/routes.py (`ConsoleRoutes`: the 72 registrations that `rust/daemon/harness/goldens/routes.json` records and `rust/daemon/src/routes.rs` embeds), plus `POST /api/pair`, which web/api.py serves ahead of the bearer gate. Python oracle pools: test_web.py and test_web_hardening.py. Every registration needs additive real HTTP normal/error/parameter/default/identity probes; POST rows also compare durable effects. GET rows that depend on later mutable subsystems still require their response contract in phase 3; writes are phase 4.

The Rust column, after Status, says what the Rust daemon answers on master after the shared gates: `served` (a Rust handler), `partly` (the named part is served, the rest answers 501) or `501` (the declared `not_implemented` refusal; `daemon/divergences.md`, "Routes whose handlers belong to later slices" and "W2-D completion boundary"). Status keeps the register's evidence rule, the one the MCP checklist above uses: `ported` needs the Rust body and a recorded differential run that compared its answers and bank effects with Python's. The Evidence cell says where that run happened. A `served` row still `deferred` means "served, evidence pending": since 2026-10-25 the three episode curation routes and since 2026-10-27 the Console reads of `read/console.rs` and `read/maintainer.rs`, whose differential runs are local until a published nightly run (B13, D11) covers them. Audited at `75a8015e` (2026-11-01): of the 72 `ConsoleRoutes`, 59 are served and 13 answer 501 (4 GET, 9 POST); none is partly served. The seven route families declare their served registrations in `rust/daemon/src/routes/{read,board,writes,graph,maintainer,episodes,cli}.rs`; `console.rs` checks their union against `goldens/routes.json`. The separate `POST /api/pair` serves refusals and redemption (`routes/pair.rs`); its recorded-evidence status remains independent of that implementation count.
