| Shutdown deadline | Implemented (B4): one 9 s exit budget taken at the signal, inside Docker's default 10 s stop grace. Open requests are waited for until 3 s (then no longer waited for; they run on until exit, and a write one submits after that is refused), admitted writes until 6 s, background tasks until 7.5 s, the final save until 9 s, and writes still running after the save until 9 s. Python waits without limit for each (uvicorn, then the atexit save), so in the container both end at the kill. Work still running at the end is left to the exit, never cancelled: a store's eviction and insert are two transactions in both daemons, and the exit can part them as the kill would a second later | declared (B4): folding eviction and insert into one transaction is a store-path change for that path's owner; `background::tests::shutdown_ends_within_its_deadlines_when_work_is_stuck`, `store::admission_tests` |
